# Superpowers pack

The skill pack for Superpowers `7.0.2` (the plugin's `skills/` folder). It says which Superpowers skill carries out each phase of the flow and where a ShipIt rule beats a Superpowers default. The rules of ShipIt always win; a skill is followed as written except where an item below says otherwise. An item cites each skill's files by their path inside the skill's folder, with the lines of that file in that version.

## Phases

- goal and roadmap: `superpowers:brainstorming`. The owner's goal and its numbered done-when items, then the roadmap; the roadmap ends with the decision point on how slices run.
- build plan: `superpowers:writing-plans`. One build plan for each slice, in the `build-plan` form.
- make: `superpowers:subagent-driven-development`, `superpowers:test-driven-development`, `superpowers:systematic-debugging`, `superpowers:using-git-worktrees`. The maker works through the steps of the build plan in a slice worktree, test first.
- check: `superpowers:verification-before-completion`. The command is `shipit check`.
- review: `superpowers:requesting-code-review` (its checklist only) and `superpowers:dispatching-parallel-agents` (the reader first, then the informed review and the second opinion in parallel).
- fix round: `superpowers:receiving-code-review`, `superpowers:systematic-debugging`, `superpowers:test-driven-development`. Each finding is verified before it is fixed.
- ship: `superpowers:finishing-a-development-branch`, only its "verify first" step and opening the pull request.
- `superpowers:executing-plans`: only when the owner chose inline execution at the roadmap's decision point, or no subagent tool exists.
- `superpowers:using-superpowers`: how skills are found; see the override on skill priority.
- `superpowers:writing-skills`: only when a skill of ShipIt is written or changed.
- `superpowers:diagnosing-superpowers`: only when the owner asks why a session went wrong.

## Overrides

- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:149-150, 255-259; references/final-review.md:6-16) and `superpowers:requesting-code-review` (SKILL.md:14-22) · a `task` review after every `task`, with spec and quality both required, then one final whole-branch review on the most capable model with `code-reviewer.md`; a review is also asked for when stuck, before a refactor and after a complex bug fix · no review after each step of a build plan, and none in between; after the last step and a passing `shipit check`, one informed review and one second opinion run, each by a reviewer one tier above the maker (rule 1), and the reader runs first · the maker works through the whole build plan; the lead starts the reader and the two reviews at the end, and they replace the final whole-branch review.
- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:140-175; references/handling-reports.md:10-11), `superpowers:executing-plans` (SKILL.md:55-58) and `superpowers:using-superpowers` (`references/claude-code-tools.md`:18-22) · the controller picks the cheapest model that fits each role, reviewers a mid tier or above; a blocked implementer is started again on a more capable model; inline execution runs on a mid-tier session model, and an orchestrator subagent on a mid-tier model · tiers and effort come from the setup: the maker's role, the reader's role and a reviewer one tier above whoever made the work (`agents.roles`, `agents.tiers`); the maker's tier and effort stay those of the setup's maker role · name the tier's model and its effort in every dispatch, and run an inline session on the maker's tier and effort; never inherit the session's model and never choose by price; a blocked maker stops and reports to the lead.
- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (references/fix-loop.md:21-34, 59-77; references/final-review.md:18-31) · five fix rounds for each `task`, rounds 4 and 5 on a stronger model, then the controller rules on what is left (the breaker); after the final review, one fix wave, one scoped re-review and no second wave · at most `agents.max_fix_rounds` fix rounds for the slice, each ending in a check and a new informed review and second opinion; past the limit, the lead decides and records it · when the rounds are used up, the maker stops, and the lead decides on each open finding and records it in the logbook; a finding left open is a known issue with a reason (rule 3), logged with `shipit log <slice> known-issue`, and goes to the owner.
- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:19-31; references/fix-loop.md:14-19; references/handling-reports.md:12-13; references/preflight.md:20-25; `task-reviewer-prompt.md`:153-157) and `superpowers:executing-plans` (SKILL.md:32-43, 199-204) · "rulings, not stalls": the controller or the inline executor decides conflicts, gaps and a wrong build plan, logs a `Ruling` line and goes on, splits a `task` that is too large, and only four kinds of thing stop it; a finding that conflicts with the build plan's text is ruled on by the controller with the spec as the binding authority, and a finding the build plan mandates is left to the human · a change to the goal, the roadmap, a shared contract or a done-when item is the owner's to decide, never a ruling; a defect in the build plan is the planner's, and a changed build plan is a new version, reviewed again; a finding that conflicts with the build plan goes back to the planner, not to the controller · stop and ask with the `decision-point` form (`shipit template decision-point`) for what is the owner's; take a defect in the build plan, such as a step too large or a finding that conflicts with it, to the planner; every other decision goes in the logbook.
- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:27-31) and `superpowers:finishing-a-development-branch` (SKILL.md:48-51, 53-82, 84-126, 132-157) · a merge or a push is a soft stop that asks first; the finish step asks the human which base branch the work forked from, offers merge locally, push and pull request, or keep, and discards the work when asked · only the owner ships (rule 4): the finish step opens the pull request and stops; the base branch is the one the slice was made from, by `shipit start` or by hand on its parent's branch when stacked, so nothing is asked about it; no merge, no tag, no push to the base branch, no menu · run `shipit verify`, open the pull request from the slice branch, say so, and hand it to the owner.
- `superpowers:writing-plans` (SKILL.md:16), `superpowers:brainstorming` (SKILL.md:246; visual-companion.md:56-58), `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:96, 113-118; references/final-review.md:44-47) and `superpowers:executing-plans` (SKILL.md:121-131, 302-304) · the build plan goes to `docs/superpowers/plans/`, the design to `docs/superpowers/specs/`, mockups to `.superpowers/brainstorm/` with `.superpowers/` added to `.gitignore`, and a ledger with briefs to `.superpowers/sdd/`, deleted with the rest of the workspace when the final review is clean · the flow keeps its files in the store, not in the project: goal, roadmap, build plan and logbook under `<git common dir>/shipit/goals/<goal>/`; the logbook is append-only, the flow deletes nothing in the store, and only `shipit complete <goal> --confirm` removes a goal's files, after every slice and proof step has shipped · the build plan is `goals/<goal>/slices/<slice>/build-plan.md` and the logbook replaces the ledger and is kept after the review; the visual companion runs without `--project-dir`, so its files stay outside the project, and no ignore rule is added.
- `superpowers:writing-plans` (SKILL.md:21, 52-90, 163-177) · a fixed header with a tech stack and a spec line, a self-review the writer runs alone, and a spec that covers several subsystems split into several build plans · the build plan is the `build-plan` form (`shipit template build-plan`) with its done-when coverage block, checked by `shipit validate build-plan` and then reviewed by a reviewer one tier above the planner; one slice has one build plan · fill in the form's parts and validate the file; run the self-review list before that review, not in place of it; a slice that needs more than one build plan goes back to the roadmap, as a new version.
- `superpowers:writing-plans` (SKILL.md:179-204) · after writing, ask the human to review the `plan` and wait for that review, and to pick subagent-driven or native execution for it · how slices run is one decision point, asked once at the end of `shipit-roadmap`, with the lead's recommendation; the build plan is reviewed by a reviewer one tier above the planner, not by the owner (rule 1) · do not ask again for each build plan; hand it over with the answer already known, send it to that reviewer, and wait for nothing from the owner.
- `superpowers:executing-plans` (SKILL.md:8-10, 85-86, 255-291) and `superpowers:requesting-code-review` (code-reviewer.md:43-48) · inline execution, a self-review by the implementer and one whole-branch review at the end; the executor re-grades the findings, rules on the reviewer's "Declined to judge" list, fixes the Critical and Important ones in one pass with no re-review, and a finding it does not fix becomes a ruling · the maker never confirms its own work (rule 1), and the reader explains the diff first; only the reviewer grades a finding, and each fix round ends in a check and a new review · after the last step run `shipit check`, then the reader, then the informed review and the second opinion; the maker does not re-grade or set aside a finding, and fixes go back through `shipit check` and both reviews, within `agents.max_fix_rounds`.
- `superpowers:requesting-code-review` (SKILL.md:26-46; code-reviewer.md:95-99, 106-135, 143) and `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:261-270) · a reviewer gets two commit ids or a diff file and returns strengths, issues graded Critical, Important and Minor, and an assessment · a review is the `informed-review` or `second-opinion` form: findings written as Conventional Comments that the owner reads as must fix, nice to fix and must answer, a result of `approve`, `approve-after-fixes` or `rework`, and Security and Performance answered every time, with no praise · fill the form from `shipit template <kind>`, check it with `shipit validate`, and record it as the receipt.
- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (references/fix-loop.md:9-13, 49-51; references/final-review.md:14-16), `superpowers:executing-plans` (SKILL.md:273-277, 295-300) and `superpowers:requesting-code-review` (SKILL.md:45) · Minor findings, and what a re-review sees outside its scope, are deferred: noted in the ledger and the final message, and never fixed in the loop · a finding is fixed, answered, or written down as a known issue with a reason (rule 3); known issues reach the owner with the evidence at ship · log each finding that is not fixed with `shipit log <slice> known-issue` and its reason, or `shipit log <slice> answer` for a question; keep no list of deferred findings.
- `superpowers:verification-before-completion` (SKILL.md:14-48) and `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:283-284; implementer-prompt.md:124-125) · fresh command output before any claim of success; the implementer's report is the test evidence, and a reviewer does not run the tests again · the evidence for a slice is the receipt of `shipit check`; nothing counts without a receipt (rule 2), and a report of the maker is not one · run `shipit check`, then `shipit verify`; a claim of done without a valid receipt is refused, whatever the maker's report says.
- `superpowers:brainstorming` (SKILL.md:182-187, 277) · a spike the human agreed to gets no tests and no review process · a spike is a named throwaway that settles one risk of the roadmap, is never shipped, and leaves a note in the logbook · keeping its code is a new slice with its own build plan.
- `superpowers:brainstorming` (SKILL.md:22-40, 90-95, 269-283) · a quick, clear request is done at once; a partner who opts out of the questions ends the skill and its gate; after the design is approved the next step is `superpowers:writing-plans` and no other skill · done-when is written before any work, then the roadmap (`shipit-roadmap`) with its decision point, and only then a build plan for each slice · write the goal with its numbered done-when items first, whatever the size; after the goal run `shipit-roadmap`, not `superpowers:writing-plans`; write a build plan only for a slice of an approved roadmap.
- `superpowers:brainstorming` (SKILL.md:52-80, 106-107, 156, 263-265, 302-308; visual-companion.md:115-118, 275) · questions to the human are free-form, asked one at a time in the session's own words; the builder check's questions go to the human in one message, and the visual companion is offered with "This might be easier if I show you. Want me to open a browser tab with some mockups?", and each mockup screen asks its own question and the user is asked to respond in the terminal · every question the owner must answer, those two included, is a `decision-point` form, the offer of the visual companion too, never the free-form line "This might be easier if I show you. Want me to open a browser tab with some mockups?"; there is no free-form question, except that feedback on a mockup the owner chose to see is not a question that needs the form, and any decision taken from it is a decision point; each form has What, Context, Recommendation and Next, within `owner.max_lines` lines (a fenced block whose info string is `example` does not count there; its lines, fence lines included, count toward `owner.example_max_lines`) · write it from `shipit template decision-point` and check it with `shipit validate decision-point`.
- `superpowers:diagnosing-superpowers` (SKILL.md:23-28, 52-53, 56-60, 64, 101-103) · intake asks free-form questions, one at a time, until the problem is stated, and the redaction level of a bundle is asked in free form · every question the owner must answer, the redaction level included, is a `decision-point` form · write each intake question from `shipit template decision-point` and check it with `shipit validate decision-point`.
- `superpowers:using-git-worktrees` (SKILL.md:41-45, 51-57, 63-100, 104-119, 130), `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:105-106) and `superpowers:executing-plans` (SKILL.md:112-113) · work may start on the main or master branch with the human partner's consent; ask before making a worktree; a native worktree tool comes first and picks the folder and the branch; else the worktree goes in `.worktrees/` at the project root, with an ignore rule added and committed when it is missing; when the sandbox refuses, work in the current folder; set up with `npm install`, `pip install -r requirements.txt` or `poetry install`; ask when the baseline tests fail · a slice always has its own worktree on its own branch and commits only the files it owns, so an ignore change is a slice of its own; no work on the base branch, with or without consent; never install globally · the lead makes the worktree with git as `shipit start` prints it, `git worktree add ../wt/<slice> slice/<goal>/<slice>`, whatever native tool exists, and asks no one; with no worktree, or with failing baseline tests, the maker stops and reports to the lead; set up only with the project's own tools.
- `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` (SKILL.md:235) · never run several implementers in parallel, because they conflict · slices run in parallel, each in its own worktree, when the roadmap says they may (their `owns` do not overlap); inside one slice there is one maker · the caps are `agents.parallel.slices` for slices and `agents.parallel.agents` for agents at once.
- `superpowers:using-superpowers` (SKILL.md:20, 26-31, 63-65) · process skills come first, and the skill check comes before any answer · the ShipIt skill of the phase comes first (`shipit`, `shipit-roadmap`, `shipit-slice`, `shipit-test-drive`), then the Superpowers skills named in Phases carry it out · when a rule of ShipIt and a Superpowers default disagree, follow the rule.
- `superpowers:dispatching-parallel-agents` (SKILL.md:12-14) · one agent for each independent problem, all started together · the reader runs first and alone, on the diff without the goal; then the informed review (given the reader's explanation) and the second opinion (given nothing but the work) run in parallel · start the three in that order and pass the explanation only to the informed review.
- `superpowers:systematic-debugging` (SKILL.md:165, 210) · when stuck, ask for help; after failed fixes, stop and discuss with the human partner before trying more · the number of fix rounds is `agents.max_fix_rounds`, and a question to the owner is a `decision-point` form, never an open talk · the maker stops and reports to the lead; past the limit, the lead decides and records it, and a finding left open is a known issue with a reason (rule 3); ask the owner only with the form, and only when the goal or a shared contract must change.
- `superpowers:systematic-debugging` (SKILL.md:172-177), `superpowers:test-driven-development` (SKILL.md:319) and `superpowers:writing-plans` (SKILL.md:132-137) · a failing reproduction comes first, and it may be a one-off script; the test and the fix are committed together once the test passes · a bug fix starts with a test that reproduces the bug, committed failing before the fix · add the reproducing test to the project's tests, commit it while it fails, then commit the fix that makes it pass.
- `superpowers:test-driven-development` (SKILL.md:24, 312, 330) · an exception to test first needs the human partner's permission, and a test that cannot be thought of is asked about · every owner question goes through the `decision-point` form, and the build plan already names the test for each step · do not ask; if a step has no test, write that in the logbook as a gap in the build plan and take it to the planner, or to the owner with the form.
- `superpowers:receiving-code-review` (SKILL.md:40-57, 76-84, 113-129, 174) · stop and ask the human partner when an item is unclear, cannot be verified or conflicts with earlier decisions, and push back in conversation on a finding thought wrong · a finding is never deleted: it is fixed, answered, or written down as a known issue with a reason (rule 3); only a change to the goal, the roadmap, a shared contract or a done-when item goes to the owner, with the `decision-point` form · verify each finding before fixing it; give a finding that is unclear, cannot be verified or seems wrong back to the lead with the reason, and the lead logs it as a known issue or asks the owner with the form.
- `superpowers:writing-skills` (SKILL.md:12, 384-386) · a personal skill lives in the runtime's skills directory, `~/.claude/skills/`; commit the skill and push it to a fork, and think about a pull request upstream · a skill of ShipIt lives in the project's `skills/` folder, never in the harness's home skills folder (`~/.claude/skills/`), and changes in a slice like any other file, and only the owner ships (rule 4) · commit in the slice worktree only; no push to a fork and no pull request upstream.
