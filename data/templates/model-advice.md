## Form

```toml
kind = "model-advice"
outcome = "ready-for-the-owner"
input = "a question about which model to use, with sources"
```

[project · slice · phase]

**What:** one line: the model choice the owner must make.
**Context:** what the choice affects and what the owner needs to know, in plain words.
**Recommendation:** the model and effort you advise, and what the other options cost.
**Sources:** one item per source, each a link starting with https:// or http://.
**Next:** what happens after the owner answers. Keep this part last.
