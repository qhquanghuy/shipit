[project · slice · phase]

**What:** one line: the choice the owner must make.
**Context:** why it comes up now and what the owner needs to know, in plain words.
**Recommendation:** what you advise, and why; say what the other options cost.
**Next:** what happens after the owner answers. Keep this part last.
